Match pairs, dig up treasures and travel the ancient world.

Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to saintgames.studio@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.
Tip: in the game, open Settings → Contact support — it adds your game version and device model so we can help faster.

How to play

Tap two matching tiles next to each other to clear them. Empty cells don’t block them: a pair can match in a row, a column or a diagonal, and the end of a row also sees the start of the next row. Clear the board (or collect the tiles shown at the top) to win the level.

Common questions

No pairs left — what now?
Tap +: it deals your remaining tiles again, shuffled, with a few new ones mixed in. The light bulb shows a pair that keeps the level winnable.

What are shovels for?
You earn shovels by winning levels. Use them in the camp to dig up the four finds of each city — then you travel on to the next one.

Will I lose my progress?
Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the game deletes it as well.

How do I turn off reminders?
In your device settings → Notifications → Temple Twins.
