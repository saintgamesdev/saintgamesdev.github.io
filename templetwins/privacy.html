Temple Twins — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Temple Twins (“the Game”), made by Saint Games (“we”, “us”), collects and how it is used. We keep it short and plain.

In short: the Game does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or location, and it does not require an account. Your game progress is stored only on your device. The Game shows ads and uses analytics: the partners listed in section 3 receive device and usage data.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (levels completed, coins, boosters, finds and settings such as sound and music) locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. If you delete the Game, this data is deleted too.

2. Notifications

With your permission, the Game can show reminders (for example, that a new daily puzzle is ready). These are scheduled on your device; we do not send them from a server and do not collect any data to do so. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

3. Analytics and advertising

The Game is free and is supported by advertising: optional rewarded videos and full-screen ads shown between levels. To show and measure ads, and to understand how the Game is played (for example, which levels are too hard), the Game includes software from the partners listed below. These partners collect data directly from your device.

What is collected: device identifiers (the advertising identifier, IDFA, only if you allow tracking; the vendor identifier, IDFV), IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country or region), device model, operating system, language and network type, gameplay events (such as level started and level completed), ad events (ads shown and clicked) and crash and diagnostic data. The Game does not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Partners:

  • Hypercell, our publishing partner (analytics of game events) — hypercell.games
  • Google Firebase (analytics, crash reports) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Adjust (install attribution and analytics) — adjust.com/terms/privacy-policy
  • Meta (Facebook SDK and Meta Audience Network: analytics, attribution, advertising) — facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • AppLovin MAX (ad mediation and advertising) — applovin.com/privacy
  • Ad networks connected through AppLovin MAX: Google AdMob and Google Ad Manager, Amazon Publisher Services, BidMachine, BIGO Ads, Chartboost, Digital Turbine (Fyber), InMobi, ironSource, Liftoff (Vungle), Mintegral, MobileFuse, Moloco, Ogury, Pangle, PubMatic, Smaato, Unity Ads, Verve, Yandex Ads and YSO Network. Each network processes data under its own privacy policy.
  • AppHarbr (ad quality and safety) — appharbr.com/privacy

Tracking and your choices. On iOS, the Game asks for your permission before tracking you across apps and websites owned by other companies (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, your advertising identifier is not shared and you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent before personalised advertising is used.

Why and for how long. This data is used to show and limit ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud, fix crashes and improve the Game. We do not sell your personal information for money; sharing identifiers with advertising partners may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” under some US state laws, and you can opt out by declining tracking as described above. Partners keep data according to their own retention rules; we keep analytics data only as long as it is needed for these purposes.

4. Purchases

The Game currently has no real-money purchases. If purchases are added, payment is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play; we never receive your payment details.

5. Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, by e-mail), contact us and we will delete it.

6. Contacting support

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you include in your message (the Game pre-fills the subject with the game version and device model to help us fix problems). We use this only to answer you and delete it when it is no longer needed.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under the GDPR or California under the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you. Since the Game stores progress only on your device, you can delete it by removing the Game. For any other request, e-mail us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be published here before they take effect.
