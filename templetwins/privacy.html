Temple Twins — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Temple Twins (“the Game”), made by Saint Games (“we”, “us”), collects and how it is used. We keep it short and plain.

In short: the Game does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos or location, and it does not require an account. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (levels completed, coins, boosters, finds and settings such as sound and music) locally on your device. This data is not sent to us. If you delete the Game, this data is deleted too.

2. Notifications

With your permission, the Game can show reminders (for example, that a new daily puzzle is ready). These are scheduled on your device; we do not send them from a server and do not collect any data to do so. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.

3. Analytics and advertising

The current version of the Game does not use third-party analytics or advertising services and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

If a future version adds analytics (to understand, for example, which levels are too hard) or advertising (such as optional rewarded videos), we will update this policy before that version is released and list each service and the data it uses. Where the law or the platform requires it, the Game will ask for your consent first, and you will be able to change your choice in the Game’s settings.

4. Purchases

The Game currently has no real-money purchases. If purchases are added, payment is handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play; we never receive your payment details.

5. Children

The Game is suitable for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example, by e-mail), contact us and we will delete it.

6. Contacting support

If you write to us, we receive your e-mail address and whatever you include in your message (the Game pre-fills the subject with the game version and device model to help us fix problems). We use this only to answer you and delete it when it is no longer needed.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under the GDPR or California under the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you. Since the Game stores progress only on your device, you can delete it by removing the Game. For any other request, e-mail us.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be published here before they take effect.
